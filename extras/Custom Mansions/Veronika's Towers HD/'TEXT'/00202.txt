Welcome to Veronika's Towers.You'll find two special keys in each tower-one will let you into the next tower and the other- multicolor one- you'll need in rooms near the end of the mansion. Be carefull with keys- NEVER use a multicolor key for one color door andkeep your eyes open for backpacks- you'll need them to carry around all those keys!Each tower also has a special - going back in- door. If you've missed something you can always go back.But it's a long journey- so you better find everything first time around...
