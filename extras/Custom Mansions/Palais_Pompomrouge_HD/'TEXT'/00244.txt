No! No!! NO!!!You've found it! The secret access to the treasure chambers of my neighbour, the cardinal archbishop of Paris!His Eminence and myself were the only ones who knew about this secret passage! The cardinal allows me to lock away some of my money here. And it is special money! Those bags contain the gold I need for my weekly demand of food! No! I implore you! Do not take this gold! What will I do without the truffles, the pastries, the oysters, the lobsters, the pheasants, the filets, the soufflés, the pies, the pralines, the wines, the liquors...Oh God, no! Don't touch this money! Don't make me eat potatoes or even bread!
