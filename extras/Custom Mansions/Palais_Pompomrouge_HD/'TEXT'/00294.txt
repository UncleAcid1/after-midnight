** Whimper **That... That... That was the money for my weekly supply of shoes! Eaarghhh! You impossible thing, you! How could you! Am I supposed to go hunting without a pair of deerskin boots? Am I expected to traverse the marble halls of Versailles without a pair of shiny patent leather shoes with diamond-studded gold buckles? Should I lower myself to walking on the silk carpets or the inlaid wood floors of my palaces and chateaus without gold-embroidered silk-sllippers with satin bows?
