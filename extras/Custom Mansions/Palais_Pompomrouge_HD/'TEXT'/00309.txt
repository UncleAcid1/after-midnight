How annoying!You found my secret eremitage! While other nobles have their little garden palaces build on the ground, mine is standing on marble colums, allowing for a beautiful view! How? My dear! You just have to be wealthy and powerful enough!This is where I sometimes retreat from the hard daily business of being an aristocrat to spend some intimate hours with some good old friends. Of course, the two horrible tower-shacks for the workers that are being build right next door are an eyesore, but as soon as my palace is finished, I will evacuate the scum again and tear down the towers.Come on in if you like. There's nothing to rob, unless you feel like carrying around large paintings.
