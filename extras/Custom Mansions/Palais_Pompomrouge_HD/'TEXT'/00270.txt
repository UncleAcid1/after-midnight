** Near-death rattle **OH MY GOD! Please, no! Not this gold! This is the pay for my guards and soldiers! The only people working for me that earn any real money! You cannot take this gold. Take anything! Take everything! But don't steal this gold! If my soldiers find out that I don't have any money to pay them, they will stop guarding this palace. And word will spread so all my other palaces and chateaus in France will be left unguarded as well! The scum will storm my magnificent houses! The common man will stroll about in my luxurious state appartments! The rabble will lay their dirty hands on my possessions! The thieves will steal and plunder! The animals will drink my wine and eat my food! The peasants will loll in my armcharis and sofas! The dregs of society will lie in my beds, desecrating the silken sheets and covers with their filthy carcassses! And when they are drunk, they will devastate and destroy and burn everything! I will be ruined, undone, annihilated! I, the last of one of the richest, proudest and oldest houses of France!
