Dear robber!You are underneath the palace foundation now. Even I cannot tell you what horros lurk here, because all the men I sent down here to explore never returned. There certainly isn't any gold to be found. Believe me. Go home, go to your wife and children and don't risk your life in pursuit of something you'll never find. Come on, what are you waiting for? Go home. Turn around and go home! Don't go on! Please...?
