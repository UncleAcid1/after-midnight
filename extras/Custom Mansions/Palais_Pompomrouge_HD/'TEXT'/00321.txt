"100.000 in gold for his weekly clothing bill? Does this guy have no shame? If I wasn't already raging mad at him, this would drive me over the edge. Of course, with four times as much skin to cover as the average man, the clothes do get a bit more expensive. But 100.000? The Marquis' pride and ostentation certainly comes at a price! I wish there was a way to unmask and ridicule him in front of the people...Wait a minute! Now there's an idea! I'll pay some of my tougher buddies to set a little trap on the roads of Paris for the state-carriage of this pompous poof next time he is on his way to court, fully decked out silks and furs. I gotta come up with a plan. It has to involve something like a large mudpit or lots of huge cream-pies*."[* 120 years later young US-actress Mabel Normand finds the 'Lost diaries of Jacques Moiloné'. She is instantly inspired and after talking to director Mack Sennett, the classic black and white slapstick movie is born.]
