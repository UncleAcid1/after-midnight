* Shriiieeek!*You, who are about to die, consider your next step! This is the gold I need for my weekly banquet! You cannot possibly consider robbing it! What will my guests say? The princes of royal birth! The noble ladies! The prelates! Imagine they sit down at my table and find that there is no glod-leaf swimming on the soup, no pearls hidden in the oysters, no jewels put on top of the petit-fours!The shame!The disgrace!
