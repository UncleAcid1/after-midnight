"Gasp!The Comte de Cremebrule? This kind and amiable gentleman? And I... I... I robbed him? I ruined him? Oh God! What have I done?"Jacques thinks for a second. Then he acts. He puts 500.000 of gold in one bag and the rest of the money in another small bag. Then he writes a quick note and puts it in the smaller bag, too. He puts a sign on the small bag saying "Property of His Eminence, the Cardinal-Archbishop of Paris".Then Jacques thinks of the Comte again. He wonderrs if there is any way to save the gold. But then he already hears the sound of footsteps approaching. The Marquis' men must have followed him through the palace and are now hot on his heels. Jacques drops the gold. Let the Marquis' men finish the dirty deed by removing the collected riches from the palace! Our brave robber takes a deep sigh and waits for the guards, swearing that if he should survive and be able to escape from prison, he will one day take terrible revenge on the evil Marquis...
