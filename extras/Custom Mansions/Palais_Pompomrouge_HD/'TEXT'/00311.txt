'Why would anybody put priceless carpets of purple silk into a damp cave?' I hear you ask? Well, I sometimes have to come down here myself!'But your feet don't even have to touch the ground of the cave because you are carried around in a sedan chair!' you protest? How utterly plebejan to think this way! Am I supposed to look at nothing but dirt and dust? Surely, you wouldn't mind... But a man of my descent!
