Tha Mansion Family: MIND MINESJack is leaving  his modest house in the city to find luck in the hills.He has heard about a charismatic guy who invites people to come live with him and his "family" in an underground labyrinth in the hills: the Mind Mines.But Jack has also heard some "less friendly" stories about the man and the place:some people in the city claim the place was long deserted, the family gone. A few voices even hint at darker things.....of creatures involved, of black magic and the family being not so nice at all...
