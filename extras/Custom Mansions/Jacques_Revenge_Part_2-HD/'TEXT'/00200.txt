After having left the "room with the red door" via the only possible exit, Jacques walked through the dark for a couple of minutes. There was a drop, some ladders, lots of corners and strange noises. During the walk Jacques lost his orientation and also found that his backpack with over 150.000 gold coins in it became far too heavy, so he dropped it, hoping to be able to pick it up again later. Eventually the master thief reached a corridor at the end of which he saw some light. He walked towards the light to find himself in a subterranean area of damp brown stone. The master thief is now standing there, breathing heavily, listening...There is a muddy, earthy smell in the air and again there are strange noises everywhere.Jacques wonders if the Cardinal is able to see him down here and get on his nerves with his verbal interventions...
