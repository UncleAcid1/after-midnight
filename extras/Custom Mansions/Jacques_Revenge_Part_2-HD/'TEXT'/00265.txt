Much to his surprise Jacques saw that Pompomrouge - although clad in dirty poor rags - was still as corpulent as always."I am doing quite well as you can see for yourself," the Cardinal laughed. "They keep stuffing me with delicacies because for some strange reason unknown to me, they want me to resemble the man I was three years ago. They are planning some mysterious ceremony that involves me wearing some of my most expensive robes and a trip in a gilt carriage to a large crowded place somewhere in the city. Oh boy! Am I ever excited! What...?""No... Nothing, Your Eminence...""Hehe, you just called me 'Your Eminence' and not tubbo or lardbutt!""You certainly don't mind?""Well, friends usually just call me Armand."Jacques hesitates for a second. "Well, then Armand it is!"
