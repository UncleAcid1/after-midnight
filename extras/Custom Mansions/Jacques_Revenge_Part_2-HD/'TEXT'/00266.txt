"Listen, Armand! You know you are in here for a good reason!" Jacques held on to the prison bars. "For years - if not decades - you have been a real pain in the butt and I must say that I don't blame people for hating you and cursing your name.""I know..." the Cardinal sighed."But I guess they already made you pay a little.""No kidding! They pillaged and destroyed all my palaces and chateaus. Add to that the abolition of privileges and the nationalization of all church property and what you get is a millionaire cardinal turned into a pauper behind prison bars. And let me tell you the first year was hell! You know how I lived three years ago! Magnificent palaces, golden staterooms, warm and costly robes, soft silken beds, every word from my mouth a wish to be instantly fulfilled. Life was one huge never-ending party. Now imagine this version of me in a damp, grey cell, barely covered in dirty rags, sleeping on a cold floor scarcely covered with hay, no servants, no minions..."
