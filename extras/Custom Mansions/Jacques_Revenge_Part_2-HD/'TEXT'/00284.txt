As he headed for the exit, Jacques heard the voice of the head of security: "What?""Nothing", the majordomo answered. "This... This guy who just walked by... I am sure I've never seen him before and yet he seemed strangely familiar...""You think he heard...?""Even if! He didn't see our faces, but I saw his. If I see him again, I'll slit his throat..."'Yeah, we'll see about that!' Jacques thought. 'We'll see about that...'
