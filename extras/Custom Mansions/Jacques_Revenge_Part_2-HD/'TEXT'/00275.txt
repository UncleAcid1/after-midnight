"Oh yes!" hisses the leader of the peasants. "It is too late to beg and cry! Your days - and I am not only talking to you, Cardinal - your days of glory and opulence are over! We are the rulers now! We will crush whatever is left of the old regime. We will strip you of all wealth, titles, dignity, power. And when that is accomplished, you will spend the rest of your days in prison or in hard labor! God, I can't wait to see your pampered butts sweating under the sun in a field or in a quarry!""Hey! The barrels are all in place!" A new voice, seemingly belonging to another peasant."Splendid!" The leader sounds more than satisfied. "Send in the torches and light the fuses!""Fuses?" the Cardinal says sobbing. "What fuses? What is going on? What...""Patience, Your Flatulence! You will find out in just a couple of minutes!"
