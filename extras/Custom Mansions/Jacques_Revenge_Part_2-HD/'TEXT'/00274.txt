"FUN? Our highborn guests: Stripped to the undergarments of their finery and wealth! This lovely little palace: Devastated! The beautiful country-seat of our beloved brother, the Archbishop of Toulouse: Burned to the ground! Surely even a soulless animal like you will recognize that this will have to stop now! We order you to call back your men before more damage is done!""Are you kidding me, fatso?" The leader of the robbers sounds rather amused. "Have you even recognized the sign of the times?""What are you talking about? What... What do you want?""What we want? We are living in a revolution! After Versailles this is the biggest, most magnificent palace in France, stuffed to the roof with the most valuable furniture, china, fabrics and works of art! You are a decadent, bloated, rich, lazy spendthrift. We are angry, poor peasants on a rampage! What do you think we want?""No!" The Cardinal's voice is nothing more than a whisper. "Oh God, please! No!"
