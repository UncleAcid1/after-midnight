There was an old prison guard by the name of Rene whom Jacques knew from his days in the Bastille. Jacques managed to persuade the old guy to organize a visit to the Cardinal's cell. Ever since the ruin of Pompomrouge, Jacques had wondered what had happened to his archenemy. He felt partly responsible for his downfall, partly concerned about the well-being of the man who fell from such heights to such misery and partly curious about what effect the years in prison might have had on the Cardinal. Rene finally gave in to Jacques' begging and was able to slip him into the prison one night. It was the 20th of March 1792."Come on boy!" the old prison guard hissed. "No time to waste. You don't have more than an hour!""That's more than enough old friend! Don't you worry! Hey! What's that?" The two of them just passed a huge treasure chest.
