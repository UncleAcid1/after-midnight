On the day that the entire grounds of Chateau Valcluse had been turned into a battlefield of blackened ruins and devastated gardens, the Cardinal had been taken to Paris, where the revolutionaries imprisoned him. They were desperate to find evidence that would allow them to put Pompomrouge to death as a declared enemy of the people and the revolution. Of course they didn't know that this was an impossible task since Jacques had burned all the documents. So the cardinal was kept in prison for a month, for a year, for two years...During the years he spent behind bars, the Cardinal eventuallly came to peace with his new situation. He became less dependent on the dreams about his glorious past and more aware of all the mistakes he made during his life. He also started to show more interest in the other prisoners, which he would meet during his daily walks in the prison court. Since he was an educated and entertaining man, who was able to provide interesting stories and knowledge, he slowly gained the other prisoners' trust and respect, which in turn made him more gentle and humble.
