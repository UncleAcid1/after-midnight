Suddenly the noise from the palace stops. Again Jacques can hear the voice of the leader of the mob. "Guys! If you have stripped the aristos, let them go. But these jewel-dripping, silk-covered, pompous, pampered mountains of flesh that go by the title of Bishop or Cardinal will have to stay and learn a special lesson!""Madness!" A new voice, high-pitched, melodic, dramatic. "The effrontery! He will rot in prison!""And who is this overfed, grand dame?""DAME? The Disgrace! The Sacrilege! We are the Archbishop of Toulouse, of course!""Oh! The very archbishop of Toulouse who owns a magnificent country-seat just two miles from here across the hill?""The very one! What... What is there to giggle about?"'Uh-oh!" Jacques thinks.
