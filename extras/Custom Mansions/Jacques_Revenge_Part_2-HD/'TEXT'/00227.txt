Jacques pauses a moment to enjoy the view. The band of peasants he saw earlier from up on the church (see 'Jacques' Revenge - Part 1') almost reached the main gate of Chateau Valcluse. Jacques also sees something that he didn't recognize earlier: smoke and fire in the far distance, right in the direction from where the peasants are coming. Jacques takes out his spyglass to take a closer look. He sees a beautiful, magnificent, big chateau, that is just in the process of being reduced to a pile of blackened ruins by wild and raging flames."Woah! Peasants on the warpath!" Jacques lowers his spyglass and shakes his head. "These guys definitely mean business! I hope it's not gonna get violent around here!"
