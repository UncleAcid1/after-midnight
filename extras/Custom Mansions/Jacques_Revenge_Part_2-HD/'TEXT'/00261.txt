"The Cardinal will wear these robes one more time", Rene answered. "You see, the revolutionaries are slowly running out of patience. If they don't find the evidence they are looking for soon, it is already agreed to simply construct evidence and have the Cardinal sentenced to death. I just heard that today the guillotine was adopted as the official means of execution of all enemies of the people. So it is planned to dress the Cardinal in these robes, drive him to the guillotine in a gilt carriage, and then in a dramatic procession lead him to his death.""Oh my God!" Jacques face turned pale."Yes, our new rulers will stop at nothing to make everybody understand that the old times are gone.
