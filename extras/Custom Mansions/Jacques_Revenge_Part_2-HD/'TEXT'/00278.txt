While walking the torchlit midnight streets of Paris, Jacques whistled a happy tune. Just a day ago he was looking forward to just take all the gold he robbed, settle down and don't do anything risky anymore. But he still was a master thief at heart, so that night he felt good, relieved and confident. Good, because there was yet another impossible job to be done. Relieved, because the Cardinal really seemed to be willing to make up for his past. Confident, because he knew that both he himself and the Cardinal would reach their goals.'Poor Armand', he thought. 'Considering the company he kept... All those lickspittles and minions... I am probably the only person close to a friend he had in the last decades. Man, that's depressing. But Rene told me that pretty much everybody in the prison likes the Cardinal. Everybody except the revolutionaries, of course. So when he gets to Rome and stays true to his new self, he shouldn't have any problems making real friends really quick, and he might actually end his days in peace and as a happy man...'
