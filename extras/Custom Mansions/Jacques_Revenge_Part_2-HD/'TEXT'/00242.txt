"God no!" The Cardinal doesn't seem to grow tired of shrieking. "Impossible! Unthinkable! Unspeakable! Dirty peasants defiling the palace grounds with their stinking rags and rotten corpses! And they are on their way here! Their presence alone will desecrate my robes and damage my dignity! Fast! Get me out! To the emergency exit! To the carriage! NOW!"The sound of bursting doors is followed by an even shriller wave of aristocratic screams and shrieks. Eventually silence sets in and a new voice is heard. "No one gets out of here as long as he or she still has the tiniest piece of gold, jewelry, fur or silk on his or her body!" Apparently the peasants  entered the garden palace. And apparently they had a leader."Does he know who we are?" asks the Cardinal, making a last, desperate attempt at saving himself and his possession.'Wow!' Jacques smiles. 'Pluralis majestatis! Stylish!'
