As he left the remains of the palace, Jacques just saw the last of the aristocrats being led to their carriages by some of the peasants. The noble ladies and gentlemen were indeed stripped to the very undergarments of all their jewelry and all their robes.'Wow!' Jacques thought. 'I bet that must be embarassing!'"Hey!" One peasant yelled. "Why do we let them get away?""Well, what should they do?" answered another one. "At the rate we're going, come next week, they'll be dressed in rags and sleeping under bridges, too. If they weren't imprisoned. Or thrown down a well. Maybe we should take it a bit easier with the palaces and the chateaus and also the monasteries..."'What's that?' Jacques stopped walking. 'The voice of reason?'"... If we destroy them all too fast, fun will be over too soon!""Oh man! Poor France!" Jacques sighed and walked on.
