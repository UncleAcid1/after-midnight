Being the wealthiest prelate of France, the Cardinal had been subject to the worst mischief. He was covered in apparently every single course of the banquet that originally was meant to end up in his spacious stomach, not on his robes. The multiple layers of silk and lace and fur had been treated with such fury, that the Cardinal de Pompomrouge was indeed half-naked. Even the powdered wig he wore was besmeared and roughed up as if the prelate had just come from a tavern brawl. A grotesque view to behold and - judging from the expression on the Cardinal's face - a humiliation too much to bear. Even from the distance Jacques could clearly recognize that Pompomrouge awakened from the sweetest of dreams to the cruelest of truths: On this dreadful day the Cardinal did not only suffer the worst humiliation in the presence of shocked high-born guests and mocking peasants. He also witnessed the beginning of the end of his power and glory. More than that: On this day the Cardinal witnessed the beginning of the end of the world as he knew and loved it.
