As he was heading for the main gate, Jacques passed the group with the Cardinal and the bishops. It was a bizzarre view to behold; a strange mix of humor and horror. From up close the prelates looked even more ridiculous and funny in their besmeared and shredded robes. But nobody was laughing. The peasants kept their victims in place by threatening them with pitchforks and knives. And the high and mighty bishops and their even higher and mightier host were closer to death than to life. They had not been harmed physically, but they all knew that their lives of luxury and opulence had just come to an end and that this end was only just beginning...As he passed Pompomrouge, Jacques lifted his hand and nodded."God bless you, Jacques Moilone!" mouthed the Cardinal, before the terror and grief finally got the better of him and he collapsed.
