As soon as he entered the room, the master thief heard the voice of the Cardinal."Jacques! What are you doing here? I would have expected pretty much anybody but not the man whom I have caused so much trouble and pain!""Forgiven" Jacques answered. "Not forgotten, but forgiven."The Cardinal laughed. "I don't even know how I deserve to be forgiven. Especially by you! And you did much more than forgive me! You burned the evidence that surely would have led to my death and thereby gave me a second chance! A chance to change, a chance to make up for all my evil deeds. A chance to repay at least a little... Thank you... Thank you so much!""Aww... Don't mention it. So, how's things?" Jacques stepped closer to the prison cell and took a look at the Cardinal.
