"Ahhh! There you are, Jacques!" The Cardinal's voice, sounding very amused."Aww, crap!" Jacques sighs and rolls his eyes. "What do you want?""Not much. Hey, wait a minute and let your eyes get accustomed to the light. You walked through the dark for such a long time. In the meantime let me tell you where you are. This is a complex labyrinth of tunnels hidden underneath my chateau. My servants and guards only refer to it as 'THE SECTION'...""The section?""Well... you know how people really like to make stuff sound interesting and scary by giving it mysterious names. Anyway, this area serves only one purpose; it contains the levers that open the doors to the real 'Room with the red door'. So whenever I feel the need to visit this room, I send some of my men down here about one hour ahead of time to prepare my arrival by opening these doors."
