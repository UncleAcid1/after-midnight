"We have to find the documents that prove he embezzled money, blackmailed the old archbishop and set up his peers to be thrown into prison!" the leader of the peasants shouted."I know where to find it", yelled one of the Cardinal's men."Hurry!" The leader nodded. "The evidence must not burn. Without it we'll only be able to imprison him. We need to set an example with a public execution based on a fair trial with sufficient evidence!""Execution?" Jacques shuddered and looked at the bag he was still holding. "But... Wait a minute... I mean..." He looked at the Cardinal again and saw a man who was completely broken. At that moment Pompomrouge looked up and saw Jacques. There was no reaction on both faces. Then Jacques shrugged, slowly lifted the bag, opened it and threw its contents into the fire.Jacques shuddered as he saw the relieved, grateful and gentle smile on the face of the Cardinal. Could there indeed be something like a human being be hidden inside this arrogant aristocrat?
