"I... I... I think I am going to be sick" Jacques stuttered. "I thought the old rulers were supposed to be the cruel ones?""Well, there certainly were cruel aristocrats. But at least they were honest about it. They didn't yell 'libery, equality, fraternity' just to split the skull of the next best person who doesn't fit their new world. They just said 'Do as I tell you or suffer the consequences!' Cruel, for sure, but also honest."Jacques shruggred and smiled. "Guess you could see it like that... Still, this change was needed. I just wish it had come sooner and from the side of the rulers. There always is too much destruction and bloodshed involved once the masses take business into their own hands...""I know," Rene sighed. "But there also always is too much stupidity and greed involved where the rich have to let go of a little to help the poor. So in the end the poor just come and take and the rich end up losing everything."
