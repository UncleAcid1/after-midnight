Jacques stares at the metal door and thinks of all the immense riches piled up in the Cardinal's palace. For one brief moment he pictures himself plundering this magnificent residence. Then he slowly shakes his head."Too bad I don't just steal from the rich in general but only from the rich and evil. His Eminence gave my father a job when he was unemployed and he often helps out the poor with food and clothing and even money... No, I could never do it. Bless you, cardinal. I hope your generosity really kicks in now that times are so hard for the poor..."
