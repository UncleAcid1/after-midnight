Paris, February 1789: The winter is harsh, the city is hot with bread riots and on the streets you hear hissing and hooting and even see raised fists whenever a splendid carriage or a large sedan-chair is passing. Many nobles decide to leave their magnificent city palaces and retreat to their country seats. Jacques Moiloné sees his big chance: The Marquis de Pompomrouge left his huge palace and rumor has it that there are incredible riches to be gained for the adventurer who doesn't fear the Marquis' famed collection of exotic and poisonous animals. Since Monsieur le Marquis is known to be a vain, ostentatious and greedy glutton, Jacques doesn't really feel guilty for deciding to thoroughly sack the palace. Time is of the essence though, since word of the Marquis' absence might spread among the rabble and it might only be a matter of hours before other looters will try their luck.Jacques made it to a big tree across from one of the palace balconys. Now he only has to swing along a couple of vines and he will be inside. But will he find the huge amounts of gold which the Marquis hid in his palace? And - more importantly: Will he make it out of there alive?
