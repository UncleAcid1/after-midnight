(effeminate shriek)Oh my Gaaaaahhhhd!My downfall!My annihilation!!My ruin!It cannot be true! It must not be true! Those bags contain the gold I need for my weekly clothing bill! All the furs, the silk capes, the moiree jackets, the brocade waistcoats, the tafetta trousers, the satin lounging-robes, the lace-bordered shirts, the damask underwear, the gold-embroidered slippers. Please, don't touch any of it! Otherwise I'll have to wear wool or cotton or linen, which will make me feel like an animal - or worse - I'll have to wear a piece of clothing twice, which will make me feel like a peasant!Oh God, no! If you rob this gold, it will be my death at court!
