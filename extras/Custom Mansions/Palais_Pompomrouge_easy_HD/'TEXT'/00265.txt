** OH... MY... GAHWD! **No! Nonono! Nononononono! NO!!The money for my weekly demand of indoor robes! My snow-white shirts of damask and lace! The gold-embroidered silken night-gowns! The ermine-fringed satin smoking robes with the eiderdown stuffing! The brocade night-caps with the gold tassles! Don't...! I beg you, DON'T!!!How will I feel in the magnificent state-appartments of my palace, when I have nothing but rags to wear? Oh my God, it will be as if the silken tapestries, the gilded furniture, the priceless china, the works of art, the satin curtains will laugh at me because they think I am a farmer who got lost in the palace... (* sob *)
