Jacques hesitates for a moment. Never before did he steal anything from a church or from a priest. He was raised as a Catholic and always remained faithful to God and His Church. All the priests he got to know were decent and pious men. Even the old Cardinal was a gentle and caring man, despite his wealth and power. This is why Jacques never even robbed one of those opulent bishops traveling across the country roads in their golden carriages. But now Jacques knows the other side. Now he knows the Cardinal Pompomrouge and has heard about the circle of like-minded bishops that constantly hover in his vicinity. Bloated, greedy, idle spendthrifts that only regard the church and their office as a means to become very rich very fast."Curse them all!" Jacques murmurs. "The Cardinal will fall. If the King doesn't throw him into jail, then the revolution will wipe him out. And if these bishops stand too close to him, they will be ruined too! All of them! As for the gold in this church: Since the Cardinal certainly doesn't plan to give it to the poor, let me see if I can do something about that… heh-heh!"
