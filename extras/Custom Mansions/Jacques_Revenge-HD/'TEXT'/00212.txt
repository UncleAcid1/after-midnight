"Hello again, Jacques!My guests and I are on the other side celebrating. And we really don't want you with us. After all, you still have to run the course so that one of us can be declared the winner of our little betting game. Next to that, you are a thief and the jewellery of my guests alone is worth a king's ransom.There is, of course, a way to reach the other side of the lake. It is long and dangerous and tedious. I am confident that you will find it. But whatever you do: Stay away from our party. The soldiers guarding the garden palace have order to take you into custody when they find you within 100 feet distance. So have a care!"
