"Well, Jacques, I guess you wonder what this little parcours was all about?As you might know or not, my robes are not only very priceless, but also very elaborate. It is not easy to find men who are able to not only hold up the end of my 30-feet silk train while I am walking, but also keep the correct distance, so the fabric wont touch the ground because the distance is too small or the pull is too annoying because the distance is too great. Next to that you want servants who also always have their eyes on the road ahead to direct my steps in the safest way possible. After all, who am I to lower my head?So what I do is dress up a 400 lbs. peasant oaf in robes similar to mine (I said 'similar'! There's no way I'll let any peasant even get into smelling-distance to my silks and furs, so the guy gets some burlap thrown over that only resembles my robes in shape) and have my men lead him up and down these belts and ladders. If everybody stays alive, I know I can trust these men. Easy, isn't it?"
