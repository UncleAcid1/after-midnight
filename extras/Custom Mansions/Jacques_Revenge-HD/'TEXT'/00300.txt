Suddenly Jacques sees a band of roughly 500 people on a hilltop in the distance. He grabs his telescope to take a closer look and sees a mob of peasants. From the fact that they are walking towards the chateau and are carrying sticks, pitchforks, hammers, axes and torches Jacques draws the conclusion that they might make an attempt at the Cardinal's seat of splendor. "Please!" Jacques jumps with excitement. "Come here guys! That would provide exactly the kind of distraction I am hoping for! But don't try to get serious! The Cardinal's guards are going to whoop your sorry butts!"Just as he wants to move on, Jacques sees the Cardinal's sedan chair coming to a halt outside of what looks like a little tool-shack close to the church. Pompomrouge is helped out of the sedan, the servants gather the silken masses of their master's robes and wrap the fabric around the Cardinal's right arm. With a pompous gesture Pompomrouge makes his man disappear. Then - after carefully looking left and right - he opens the door and enters the shack. "No way that spoiled glutton has any business in there unless it is connected to some secret hideaway!" murmurs Jacques. "I gotta get in there!"
