"Jacques, my dear!So you found the secret entrance from the church! I am sorry for the cold down here. I hope you brought your fur! I'd love to lend you one of my ermine capes, but as they were tailored to fit a more majestic body then your skin-coated skeleton, I'm afraid there'd just be too much surplus cape that gets in your way.As you no doubt have noticed yourself, you are in the ice-cellars now. I like my food fresh and my white wines chilled. Speaking of which: I strongly suggest you lay off the crates with the wine! Not because it would be such a shame if a lousy thief like you would slug back costly liquids made to satisfy the needs of a refined and appreciative palate like mine (actually it would be a shame) but because I don't want you to get drunk and fall asleep.After all, you still have to help me to win the bet! Come on, old buddy! Do be a sport! And hurry! My men must be hot on your heels, and I don't really want them to catch you just yet and end the fun we are having."
