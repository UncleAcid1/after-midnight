It so happened that just that very evening the Cardinal had invited the high-society to a huge social gathering. The party was taking place in one of the little picturesque garden-palaces on the vast grounds of the chateau. Jacques had paid a handful of suspicious looking fellows to hang around the grounds, close to the eremitage where the party was in full swing. Word about the presence of the rabble soon spread and the guards focussed their attention on them and thereby away from the side gates. Jacques entered the premises and was now strolling down a side-alley that lead to one of the smaller wings of the chateau...
