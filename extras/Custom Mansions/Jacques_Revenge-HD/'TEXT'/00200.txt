Ile de France, August 14st 1789.One month ago the mobs of Paris had stormed the Bastille and set the prisoners free. Among the people locked away in the old royal prison was Jacques Moiloné, master-thief. Now that he was free again, there was only one thought on Jacques' mind: Revenge! Revenge on the Marquis de Pompomrouge, who had not only managed to trick Jacques into stealing half a million in gold from the Comte de Cremebrule, but had also caused the ruin of this nobleman by paying gangs of ruffians to sack all his houses and bring him the loot and by setting up the Comte as the mastermind behind an attempt at the treasure vault of the Cardinal Archbishop of Paris. Though nobody really believed that the gentle Comte had anything to do with it, all the evidence pointed towards Cremebrule and the King had no option but to have him locked away in the Bastille. He was given a comfortable cell as well as food and clothing worthy of his rank. Initially it seemed like he took the loss of his immense fortune very well. But when he received the messages about the death of his son who was trading furs overseas and the subsequent suicide of his beloved wife, the good man's spirit finally was broken.
