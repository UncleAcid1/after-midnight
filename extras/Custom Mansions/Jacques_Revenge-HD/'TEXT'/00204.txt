Ahhh...!Jacques, dear old friend! Welcome to my humble home! I've been expecting you! So you decided to come here! That is stupid, albeit amusing. You might be smart enough to avoid my guards, maybe even my pets and the deadly traps that are waiting for any thief who is suicidal enough to enter one of my palaces. Feel free to grab all the gold you might find on the way. My guards are just going to take it back when they arrest you. It's really up to you: You can either continue and get caught or you can jump back into whatever mud-pit you crawled out of. I am busy celebrating my elevation to the cardinalate. I would have invited you but I wasn't sure you could make it, what with you being locked away in the Bastille and all. Also, my guests are people of a somewhat... - how should I put it - ... cultivated and educated nature. So while my dear friends might find the presence of an exotic little bum like you amusing for a couple of minutes, you wouldn't feel at ease amongst all the aristocrats and highest bishops of the Church of France.
