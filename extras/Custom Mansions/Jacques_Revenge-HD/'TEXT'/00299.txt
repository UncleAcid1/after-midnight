From up high Jacques sees an ornate and magnificent garden palace with a huge marble terrace in the front. About a hundred aristocratic ladies and gentlemen and a dozen bishops are standing or sitting inside and outside, chatting, laughing, eating and drinking."So, this is where Pompomrouge has his little fete..." Jacques mumbles. "The Cardinal himself doesn't seem to be there... Even among those other overfed and magnificent bishops he would stick out..." Then he sees the men he paid to create some ruckus on the other side of a gate close to the party. They are mostly just standing there, looking at the guests, occasionally shouting and yelling. Some men of the Cardinal's guard are standing inside, carefully watching the mob."Bummer! They don't nearly attract enough attention!" sighs the master thief. All of a sudden, soldiers appear behind the mob. They draw near quickly and before the men even know what happened, they are arrested and led away. "No!" Jacques yells. "Now all the guards can focus on catching me! I gotta be fast and careful!"
