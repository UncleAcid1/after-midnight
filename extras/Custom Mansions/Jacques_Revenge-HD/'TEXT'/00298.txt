"This is going to be a tough one, Jacques!You probably ask yourself what exactly this is? It's a little exercise for my sedan-chair men. Once a week I have one of the chairs filled with 400 lbs of gold, which roughly matches the weight of my anointed body. My men then have to carry the chair along this parcours without falling. If they succeed I know that I can entrust my princely person to them. If they fall, I'll make them pick up all the gold that's scattered around, subtract whatever is missing from their wage and have them build a new sedan chair, also from their money. This way they'll never forget the value of a Cardinal and they will learn to be very, very careful when carrying me."
