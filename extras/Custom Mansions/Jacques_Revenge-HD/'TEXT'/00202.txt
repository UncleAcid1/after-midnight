Jacques remembers the conversation he had with the Comte:Comte: "Valcluse! If I was a robber, sure, I could go for the gold that the Cardinal keeps there. It is quite a bit. Add to that his collection of art, china, furniture and of course the jewels and you've got an incredible fortune."Jacques: "Oh yeah!"Comte: "But I'd rather look for the room with the red door!"Jacques: "The room with the red door?"Comte: "This is where he keeps all the documents and letters that prove how he set you up, how he ruined me, how he blackmailed the old Cardinal-Archbishop into retirement so that he could become his successor..."Jacques: "BLACKMAILED THE CARDINAL?? How do you know? You were in prison with me all the time!"Comte: "I might be ruined and imprisoned, but I still have all my good friends and - more importantly - loyal spies in all the houses of the Marquis. Anyways: The documents also prove that he is embezzling large amounts of church money. So if somebody found the room and brought the documents to Versailles..."
