"Awww, crap!" Jacques yells. "Curse this bloated, pompous, arrogant, fat, vainglorious, ostentatious, greedy, rotten, filthy, stinking, lazy son of a...""Hey!" hisses the cardinal. "I am listening, pipsqueak!""Oh, is that so, lardbutt?" Jacques raises a fist and shakes it into the direction from which the voice is coming. "Then listen well: Your haughtiness and carelessness will be your downfall! Don't forget that so far I found all the gold you thought was safely hidden! Why should I not find the bag?""Well, go ahead and find it, punk!" sighs the cardinal. "I said it before and I'll say it again: My men are going to take it from you anyways.""We'll see about that," Jacques murmurs. And again, louder: "We'll see about that!"
