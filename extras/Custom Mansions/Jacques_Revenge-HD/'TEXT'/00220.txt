When Cardinal Pompomrouge was informed about the destruction of his Parisian palaces, he staged a quick and dramatic opera of shrieking, whining and almost dying, before he ordered the ruins to be removed and a new palace to be build. "With every disaster there comes a chance!" he proclaimed. "Now I can build one homogeneous palace instead of connecting two that didn't really match anyways." The Cardinal obviously did not think that the events in Paris might point towards something bigger and even more catastrophic...And while the National Assembly was quickly working to bring about the end of the 'ancien regime', the abolition of the privileges of the aristocracy and the clergy and the ruin of the nobles and the prelates, Jacques took a horse and rode to Valcluse. On his way he saw a chateau being attacked by peasants. He wondered if Valcluse would still be standing when he arrived. After all, it was the famed seat of opulence of one of the most-hated aristocrats in France...
