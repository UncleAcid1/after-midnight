While I am celebrating, my private army is already marching towards Paris to stifle this little annoying ruckus that people refer to as the "Revolution". This scum dared to destroy both my private palace and my archiepiscopal residence, thereby annihilating immeasurable riches. This will not go unpunished. But I will not be as cruel to them as they deserve. After all, a cardinal has to be an example of Christian charity. So they will not die. They will just spend the rest of their days in forced labor, erecting my new palace where the two old ones stood. I thereby give those poor souls a chance to make up for their evil deeds. Well, enough chatter, you can go back now...Just kidding: You cannot go back. As soon as my guards, who were expecting you, saw you entering, they took position at all the gates of the chateau grounds. So please be a dear and just wait here to be picked up by my men, will you?
