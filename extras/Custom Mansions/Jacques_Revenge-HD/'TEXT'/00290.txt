A deafening, shrill shriek is heard and Jacques instantly recognizes the Cardinal: "Oh my God! You animal! What have you done! My vestments! My priceless robes! Ruined! Torn to shreds! This is it! The game is over! You better run for your life because as of now my men will be hot on your heels. And when they take you...""Hey!" Jacques grins. "Finally a life-sign from the fat pompous sissy! Should have known that trashing his precious possessions would make him whine and shriek. Well, I think I better hurry now! Time to find the room with the red door, collect the evidence and scram!"
