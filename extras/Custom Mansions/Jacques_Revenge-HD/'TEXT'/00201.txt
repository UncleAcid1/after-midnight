The old Cardinal Archbishop of Paris had retired in March. Pompomrouge, a canon of the Cathedral of Notre-Dame, greased the wheels, made a generous donation to Rome and was installed as Archbishop of Paris on the 12th of April. Only one month later he went to Rome, where he was made a Cardinal. Upon his return to France, the Marquis found himself to be among the ten wealthiest noblemen of France... He quickly made it into the top-five after starting to embezzle huge amounts of church-money that was supposed to go into the fond for the old and sick priests...Jacques and the Comte de Cremebrule became friends in prison and the fallen nobleman told the robber a very interesting secret: Ever since the bread riots in Paris and the assembly of the states general, Pompomrouge had decided it was safer to leave the capital. He went to Chateaux Valcluse, his largest and most magnificent country-seat...
